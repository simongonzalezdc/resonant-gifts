## Lexicon — the ecosystem's terms, plainly

Plain-English restatements, attributed to their canonical sources. The
sources win on any disagreement.

- **Contextual authority** — weight follows verified outcomes in the exact
  dimensions a question declares salient, never a blanket score, never
  capital, never activity. The whitepaper states the mechanism: "Each
  governance question declares its salient dimensions, and voting weight
  follows verified balances."
  `Source: ResonantDAO whitepaper, https://resonantdao.com/whitepaper`
- **Contribution economy** — the ecosystem's own phrase for what it is
  building: "a community-owned contribution economy built around ResonantOS"
  that rewards recorded contribution across many dimensions instead of one
  transferable score. `Source: ResonantDAO whitepaper; Contribution Economy
  specification, https://resonantdao.com/contribution-economy/`
- **The 22** — the economy measures contribution across 22 dimensions named
  after the Major Arcana of the Tarot; a member holds 22 non-transferable
  contribution balances (C_0 through C_21), one per dimension. The full
  dimension spec lives in their Contribution Economy specification.
  `Source: ResonantDAO whitepaper, https://resonantdao.com/whitepaper`
- **$RES** — the transferable Phase 1 economy and reward token of the
  ecosystem, with a planned supply of 1,000,000,000.
  `Source: ResonantDAO whitepaper, https://resonantdao.com/whitepaper`
- **$RCT** — the Resonant Contribution Token: the derived overall summary of
  a member's 22 normalized balances. It is non-transferable and cannot be
  bought, sold, or transferred.
  `Source: ResonantDAO whitepaper, https://resonantdao.com/whitepaper`
